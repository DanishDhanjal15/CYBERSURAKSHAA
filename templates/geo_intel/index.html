How to Read

Each pin is an artefact this platform assessed, placed at the coarsest honest estimate of where its infrastructure sits.

📱 Phone pins use the number's issuing telecom circle. Portability, roaming and VoIP all break this; treat it as a lead, never as a location.

🌐 Domain pins use IP geolocation of the hosting server. CDNs and proxies mean this is frequently the CDN's city, not the operator's.

⚠️ Grey pins could not be geolocated at all. They sit on a metro centroid so they appear in the list; their position means nothing.
